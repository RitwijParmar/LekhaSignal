# Three-minute portfolio demo

1. **Start with the business stake.** “Finance needs the 08:00 revenue close. LekhaSignal shows whether its data is safe to trust before a dashboard is published.”
2. **Show the platform.** Point out the distinct responsibilities: Snowpipe/CDC capture, Streams and Tasks for an audited merge, dbt plus Dynamic Tables for revenue marts, and contract/reconciliation controls.
3. **Ask naturally.** Enter: “Why did cash reporting double-count payments today?”
4. **Walk the evidence packet.** Show duplicate rate, replayed offset range, affected governed assets, and the financial exposure.
5. **Show the agent boundary.** The Detector, Lineage Investigator, and Impact Analyst are done; Recovery Planner is explicitly “approval required.” No data has changed.
6. **Close with the operational loop.** “An operator approves an audited quarantine/replay. LekhaSignal reruns reconciliation and only then permits the mart exposure to publish.”

Do not claim the synthetic incident metrics are a customer result. Describe the demo as a controlled training scenario.
